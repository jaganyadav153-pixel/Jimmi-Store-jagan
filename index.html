Frequently asked questions

What are delivery charges & times?

Standard 60-min is Free above ₹99, otherwise ₹25. Express 30-min is ₹25. We deliver 7 AM – 11 PM. Slots held 90 min while you shop.

How do I use coupons?

Use FRESH10 (10% up to ₹100), SAVE50 (₹50 off), FREEDEL (free delivery) at checkout. Apply one per order.

Return policy?

100% satisfaction guarantee — report missing/damaged within 48 hours for auto-refund. Fresh produce fully refundable, baby formula/alcohol cannot be returned per law.

Do you accept EBT SNAP / COD?

Yes: EBT SNAP for eligible foods (fees via secondary card), plus UPI, cards, wallets and Cash on Delivery.
